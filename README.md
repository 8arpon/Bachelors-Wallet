## 🌟 Detailed Feature Breakdown

### 1. 🌐 In-App Auto-Update Engine (GitHub Releases CDN)
* **Serverless Free Distribution:** Fetches releases directly from `https://api.github.com/repos/8arpon/Bachelors-Wallet/releases/latest`.
* **Live Download Progress:** Clean in-app modal with real-time download percentage progress indicator, markdown changelog viewer, and automatic package installation trigger via Android `FileProvider`.
* **Manual & Auto Checks:** Automatically verifies updates upon app launch, with an on-demand "Check for Updates" tile in **Settings $\rightarrow$ About**.

### 2. 🏠 Mess & Hostel Manager (Collaborative Bachelor Living)
* **Roommate Directory:** Add and manage roommates with contact details and individual deposit histories.
* **Daily Meal Matrix:** Record and monitor daily meal consumption (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner counts per member).
* **Bazaar & Dynamic Meal Rate:** Automatically computes real-time meal rate with transparent cost allocation:
  $$\text{Meal Rate} = \frac{\text{Total Shared Bazaar Expense}}{\text{Total Meals Consumed}}$$
* **Fixed Shared Expenses:** Distribute apartment rent, chef/cook salary, WiFi, utilities, and electricity fairly among all roommates.
* **Individual Settlement Ledger:** Clear transparency showing exactly who owes money to the mess and who is entitled to a refund at month end.
* **Wallet Integration:** Auto-deduct personal mess bazaar contributions and deposits directly from your primary wallet balance.

### 3. 🤖 Google Gemini AI Financial Assistant & Roasting
* **Conversational AI Chatbot:** Multi-turn interactive chat giving personalized advice on expense cutting, mess budget optimization, and student savings.
* **AI Roasting & Smart Alerts:** Context-aware prompts that analyze your daily burn rate and playfully roast or warn you when exceeding safe limits.
* **Rich Markdown Formatting:** Math formulas, financial tips, bullet points, and code blocks rendered directly in Compose.

### 4. 📊 Intelligent Budget Pacing Algorithm & Safe Daily Spend
* **Dynamic Pacing Algorithm:** Continuously calculates your spending velocity against the progression of the month:
  $$\text{Month Progress} = \frac{\text{Current Day}}{\text{Total Days in Month}} \quad \text{vs} \quad \text{Budget Progress} = \frac{\text{Total Spent}}{\text{Spendable Budget}}$$
  Provides real-time feedback: *Great Pacing*, *Over-pacing Warning*, or *Critical Burn Rate*.
* **Safe Daily Spend:** Dynamically recalculates how much you can spend per day based on remaining days in the month:
  $$\text{Safe Daily Spend} = \frac{\text{Spendable Balance} + \text{Today's Spent}}{\text{Days Remaining in Month}}$$
* **Monthly Savings Goal Lock:** Set aside a protected savings target that is automatically deducted from spendable balance before computing daily allowances.
* **Tomorrow's Budget Prediction:** Real-time visibility into how saving money today will boost your allowance tomorrow.
* **Category Budget Caps:** Individual spending thresholds with color-coded warning indicators (Green $<70\%$, Orange $70-90\%$, Red $>90\%$).
* **Built-in Math Expression Parser:** Type arithmetic expressions (e.g. `50+30*2-15`) directly into transaction inputs.

### 5. 🤝 Advanced Debt & Loan Tracker (IOU)
* **Dual-Direction Ledger:** Visualizes "I Owe" (borrowed cash) vs "They Owe" (lent cash) with color-coded progress bars.
* **Wallet Balance Sync:** Toggle whether borrowed or lent transactions immediately affect your total wallet balance.
* **Settlement Workflows:** Record partial payments or mark debts as fully settled with single-tap ease.

### 6. 👑 Bachelors PRO Subscription & Coupon Engine
* **Flexible Tiers:** 1 Month, 6 Months, 1 Year, and Lifetime access plans.
* **Promo Code Engine:** Redeem custom coupon codes (e.g. `FREEPRO`, `BACHELOR2026`) for instant tier activation.
* **PRO Privileges:** Unlocks the Gemini AI Assistant, full Mess Manager tools, automatic background cloud synchronization, and exclusive luxury themes.

### 7. ⏰ Repeating Transactions & Scheduled Bills
* **Recurring Automation:** Schedule recurring monthly expenses like House Rent, WiFi, Tuition Fees, and Subscriptions.
* **Auto-Processing:** Automatically logs due transactions whenever the application is opened.

### 8. 🎨 Dynamic Multi-Theme Engine
* **System-Aware:** Seamless dark and light mode adaptation.
* **7 Curated Color Palettes:** Modern Slate, Emerald Green, Soft Rose, Midnight Purple, Royal Gold (PRO), Cyberpunk Neon (PRO), and Forest Pine (PRO).

### 9. ☁️ Firebase Cloud Backup & Biometric Security
* **Firebase Authentication:** Google Sign-In and Email/Password authentication.
* **Cloud Firestore Sync:** 1-tap backup and restore across multiple Android devices.
* **Biometric Authentication:** Secure critical actions (such as complete data wipe or budget configuration modification) with fingerprint or device lock.

### 10. 📑 Export & Reporting
* **PDF Financial Statements:** Generate clean, formatted monthly statements and category-wise breakdowns for records or expense sharing.
